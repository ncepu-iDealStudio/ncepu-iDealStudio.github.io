In August 2023 the project added the `config` and `cmd` modules and assigned them to the newly joined **Dou Xingwang** and **Wang Yujie**. `config` is where configuration enters, `cmd` carries the command line and the start-up flow; together they are the foundation every later feature hangs from — fix these two first and other modules have somewhere to sit.

It was also the first time the team worked by "module plus owner": clear boundaries, interfaces first, and progress running in parallel. For a project group one month old, that way of splitting work shaped the pace more than the number of lines written.
