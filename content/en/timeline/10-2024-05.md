In May 2024 Fast-Https entered the 2023–2024 undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship programme of North China Electric Power University and, after the mid-term review, was rated national-level excellent.

For the team it was more than a medal. The programme demanded clear goals, progress, ownership and staged results, which forced the team to turn "what we did" into material an outsider could verify — material later reused for competition entries and community outreach.
