On 25 May 2024 Fast-Https passed the review of Huawei's openEuler technical committee and officially joined the openEuler community. The record appears in the [openEuler community operations report for May 2024](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/zlGh9D6_vZiIvHBGVLQJ0A).

Joining placed the project inside the domestic operating system ecosystem: code has to survive reading by outside developers, contributions have to follow the community's process, and questions have to be answered promptly. For a student team it marked the turn from "we build it, we use it" to "others can use it and will have opinions".
