At the end of July 2023 the project gained new members: **Dou Xingwang** and **Wang Yujie** from computing class 2102 joined the studio. Together with **Zhang Yihe**, **Liu Haoyu**, **Peng Bo** and **Cheng Kai**, the founding team of Fast-Https was complete.

For a project that had just started, people were scarcer than code. Members had different strengths — some closer to request handling and low-level implementation, some more familiar with business scenarios and deployment — and only with that mix could the work be split. The roster mattered because from then on new modules, tests, multi-platform packaging and releases had stable owners.
